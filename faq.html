Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome, but calling ahead at 508-548-0136 helps us get you in and out faster.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes — every repair at Falmouth Auto Center is backed by a 12-month warranty.

Do you provide estimates before starting work?

Always. We quote you before any work begins — no surprises.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, check and credit cards.

How long will my repair take?

It depends on the job. We'll give you a time estimate up front and keep you posted along the way.

Do you sell used cars?

Yes. We buy, inspect and sell quality used cars — each one shop-inspected before sale and backed by a warranty. Call 508-548-0136 to ask what's on the lot.

What kinds of vehicles do you work on?

We service most cars, trucks and SUVs. If you're not sure, just give us a call.

Do you handle state inspection questions?

The best way to find out what we can take care of for your vehicle is to call us at 508-548-0136.

Where are you located?

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Saturday 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM, and closed Sunday.

Do you speak Portuguese?

Yes — we speak both English and Portuguese.

How do I book an appointment?

Call or text 508-548-0136, or use our online quote form and we'll get right back to you.
