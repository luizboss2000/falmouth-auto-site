Tires are where your car meets the road, so they're not the place to cut corners. We carry and order all the major brands, mount and balance them properly, and give you a straight recommendation based on how and where you drive on the Cape.

Cape Cod is tough on vehicles — salt air, summer traffic and cold winters all take a toll. Small problems have a way of becoming expensive ones if they're ignored, and staying ahead of them is a lot cheaper than waiting for a breakdown on Route 28 in July.

Signs it's time to come in

  • Tread looks low or wears unevenly
  • Tire-pressure light is on
  • Vibration or pulling while driving
  • Visible cracks, bulges or a slow leak
  • It's been years since your last set

Noticing any of these? Call 508-548-0136 and we'll take a look.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you sell new tires?

Yes — we carry and order all major brands, and we'll help you pick the right tire for your car and budget.

Can you fix a flat?

In many cases, yes. If the puncture is repairable we'll patch it; if it's not safe to repair, we'll tell you straight.

Do I need winter tires on Cape Cod?

Good all-season tires handle most Cape winters, but if you drive in heavy snow, dedicated winter tires add grip. We'll give you an honest recommendation.

How much are new tires?

It depends on your vehicle and the brand you choose. Call 508-548-0136 for a quick quote.
