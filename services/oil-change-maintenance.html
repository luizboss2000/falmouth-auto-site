Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I change my oil?

It depends on your vehicle and the type of oil. Conventional oil is usually every 3,000 to 5,000 miles, and full synthetic every 7,500 miles or more. We'll check your owner's manual and set the right interval for you.

Do you use synthetic oil?

Yes. We offer conventional, synthetic blend and full synthetic, and we'll recommend what your engine actually needs — nothing you don't.

How long does an oil change take?

Most oil changes are quick. Call ahead at 508-548-0136 and we'll let you know the best time to come in.

Will you tell me if something else needs work?

We do a multi-point check with every oil change and show you anything we find. We never push repairs you don't need — that's how we've kept customers for 19 years.
