Frequently Asked Questions

What does a check-engine light mean?

It can be anything from a loose gas cap to a failing sensor or a bigger engine issue. We scan the codes and diagnose the real cause so you know exactly what you're dealing with.

Can you work on my transmission?

Yes. We handle transmission service and many transmission repairs. Bring it in and we'll diagnose it first.

Is it safe to drive with the light on?

If the light is steady and the car drives normally, it's usually okay to drive to us soon. If it's flashing, stop driving and call us at 508-548-0136.

How much will the repair cost?

We diagnose first, then give you a clear quote before any work begins. No surprises.
