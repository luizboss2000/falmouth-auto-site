Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026 · Falmouth Auto Center, East Falmouth, MA

Information we collect

When you request a quote or opt in to text messages, we collect your name, mobile phone number, optional email, and details about your vehicle and service request.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request and schedule service.
  • To send appointment confirmations and service reminders (oil, tires, brakes) if you opt in.
  • To send occasional offers, only if you opted in.

SMS / text messaging

By providing your mobile number and opting in — in person at our shop, on your repair order, through a form or request on this website, or verbally — you agree to receive recurring automated text messages from Falmouth Auto Center at the number provided, including appointment confirmations, vehicle service and maintenance reminders (such as oil, tires, and brakes), service follow-ups, and occasional offers. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

Mobile information & third parties

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We may share information only with subcontractors that support our own services (for example, our text-message provider), strictly to operate our business and never for their own marketing.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We use trusted service providers (for example, to send messages or store records securely) only to operate our business.

Your choices

You may opt out of text messages by replying STOP. To request access to or deletion of your information, contact us at falmouthautocenter@yahoo.com.
