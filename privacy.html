Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026 · Falmouth Auto Center, East Falmouth, MA

Information we collect

When you request a quote or opt in to text messages, we collect your name, mobile phone number, optional email, and details about your vehicle and service request.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request and schedule service.
  • To send appointment confirmations and service reminders (oil, tires, brakes) if you opt in.
  • To send occasional offers, only if you opted in.

SMS / text messaging

By opting in, you agree to receive text messages from Falmouth Auto Center. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. We do not sell or share your phone number or SMS consent with third parties for their marketing.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We use trusted service providers (for example, to send messages or store records securely) only to operate our business.

Your choices

You may opt out of text messages by replying STOP. To request access to or deletion of your information, contact us at falmouthautocenter@yahoo.com.
